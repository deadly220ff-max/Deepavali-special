The five days

Deepavali is celebrated over five days, each with its own custom.

  1. DhanterasHomes are cleaned and new utensils or gold are bought for prosperity. Lamps are lit for Dhanvantari and Kubera.
  2. Naraka ChaturdashiAn oil bath before dawn and the first lamps at the door. Marks the defeat of the demon Narakasura.
  3. Lakshmi PujaThe main night. Rows of diyas, rangoli at the threshold, and prayers to Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Ganesha.
  4. Govardhan PujaNew Year in many communities. Families offer a large spread of food and visit the temple.
  5. Bhai DoojSisters pray for their brothers, put a tilak on their foreheads, and share a meal together.
